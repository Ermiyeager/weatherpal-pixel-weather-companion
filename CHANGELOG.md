# Changelog

All notable changes to this project are listed here.

## [0.2.0] – 2026-11-06
### Added
- Live weather from the Open-Meteo API, with Addis Ababa, Ethiopia as the default location.
- Time of day from the location's real sunrise and sunset.
- Live hourly forecast and tomorrow's outlook.
- City search with the Open-Meteo Geocoding API.
- Refresh every 15 minutes, retries with increasing delays, and an offline copy of the last result.
- "Live" option alongside the preview weather states.

## [0.1.0] – 2026-11-06
### Added
- First clickable prototype of the compact desktop companion.
- Seven weather environments, four times of day and crossfade transitions.
- Layered 16 × 24 pixel character with an automatic outfit engine and a manual mode.
- Character Studio: name, skin tone, hairstyle, hair color and top color.
- Idle animations, click reactions and rule-based speech bubbles.
- Celsius and Fahrenheit, light and dark themes, settings saved in the browser.
